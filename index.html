TOP STORY

THOR says the expected RV-market rebound never really arrived

THOR Industries reported fiscal-2026 sales of $9.61 billion, essentially flat year over year, while net income fell 31.3%. Fourth-quarter revenue declined 8.4% and net income dropped sharply as affordability pressures continued to weigh on the North American RV market.

The details RV owners and shoppers should watch: North American towable sales were down heavily in Q4, while dealer inventories were lower year over year — a healthier setup for the 2027 model year.

BUSINESS & MARKET

Interest is still there. Affordability is the problem.

Strong consumer turnout at Hershey sits alongside softer manufacturer profitability and dealership performance. That combination suggests RV enthusiasm has not disappeared, but higher purchase prices, financing costs, fuel and ownership expenses continue to make buyers cautious.

What to watch next: 2027 MSRP strategy, dealer ordering levels, lower-priced floorplans, and whether manufacturers standardize more lithium, solar and connectivity without pushing payments even higher.
